About us! Tracky Rafting

Tracky Rafting is an adventure company dedicated to providing exciting and unforgettable white water rafting experiences. Our experienced guides help visitors safely explore beautiful rivers while enjoying the thrill of the rapids. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced adventurer, Tracky Rafting is ready to make your next river trip an amazing experience.

History

Tracky Rafting was founded in Colombia in august 2000 by a group of adventure enthusiasts who wanted to share the excitement of white water rafting with more people. What started as a small local business quickly grew as more visitors discovered the beauty and thrill of Colombia's rivers. Over the years, Tracky Rafting has continued to grow, welcoming families, friends, and adventurers from around the world while keeping its passion for nature, safety, and adventure.
